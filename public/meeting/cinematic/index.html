About the footage and performances

The Unity environment footage was captured from Days Gone Quiet. The moving team sequence is the existing scripted mission demonstration, identified on screen. It is not a learned-policy evaluation. Filming used a separate project copy with camera adjustments and cutaway roofs.

The AEGIS command center and headquarters are fictional future-vision scenes. They illustrate the direction of the story; they do not claim a deployed military system or demonstrated transfer from Unity. Athena and Rowan are fictional characters with prepared, audio-driven performances. Rowan’s new face and synthetic voice were designed as an original character inspired by casting references. This preview does not listen for audience questions.
